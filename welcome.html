Why this exists

Commercial UMPJE question banks are useful but not always current or accurate — federal pharmacy law changes, and some widely-circulated practice questions lag behind it. This tool's content was checked line-by-line against DEA and FDA primary sources (21 CFR, DEA guidance, FDA compliance pages), with the places where common study material disagrees with current federal rules flagged explicitly rather than silently corrected.

Before you rely on this: this is an independent, personal study project — it is not produced, reviewed, or endorsed by NABP, and it is not a substitute for NABP's own official UMPJE materials or your state board of pharmacy's requirements. Federal pharmacy law also changes over time; treat anything here as a study aid, not a legal reference, and verify anything exam-critical against current primary sources.

How it works

Everything — the question bank, the cram sheet, your progress — lives in your browser. Nothing is sent to a server, there's no login, and once you've opened it once, it keeps working with the internet off. Add it to your phone's home screen for a full-screen, app-like experience.
